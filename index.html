FAQs

Everything you'd ask a counsellor.

Who's eligible to upgrade to Neovarsity?+

Anyone with an undergraduate degree in any field and at least one year of professional experience. Diploma-holders: case-by-case - talk to a counsellor.

How long is the program?+

18 months from the date of enrolment. Extendable up to 24 months if you use a Pause/Reset. Beyond that requires program admin approval.

When do I get access to the modules?+

Immediately on enrolment. All degree modules unlock the moment your enrolment is confirmed.

Is the curriculum different from regular Scaler?+

Your Scaler curriculum stays exactly the same. Neovarsity adds on top - additional electives, live AI/ML classes, and the modules that earn you the Woolf credits.

What additional modules do I get?+

Electives include: ML Foundations (Python, Probability & Stats, Core, Product Analytics), Data Engineering 1 & 2, DSA for Competitive Programming, Product Management for Engineers, and the unchosen specialisation from your base program. Plus recorded modules in Advanced Programming, Low-Level Design, Advanced Software Engineering, Backend Project, and High-Level Design.

What does the upgrade cost?+

There's a single upgrade fee that covers degree documentation, the live program extension, additional electives, and extended placement support. Counsellors share the exact number on your 1:1 call - it varies slightly by program and batch.

Is the Master's degree recognised in India?+

Woolf is a foreign institute and isn't recognised by UGC/AICTE. But the degree is ECTS-accredited and ECE-approved - recognised globally, including by Indian employers and most international universities. The same rule applies to degrees from foreign institutions like Oxford.

Can I use the degree for a PhD or further studies?+

Yes. 90 ECTS credits are transferable toward further academic programs in most of the world.

Will Neovarsity add to my Scaler workload?+

No. The core idea is no extra effort - you complete the Scaler modules you'd be doing anyway, and they count toward the Master's. The additional electives are how you specialise.

What is the Oxford certification, and is it included?+

It's the 'Advanced Business Analytics with AI' certification from Oxford. It's optional but recommended. You get 1 academic credit, official Oxford alumni recognition, hands-on case studies, a live session with ex-MIT faculty, and a final AI project.
